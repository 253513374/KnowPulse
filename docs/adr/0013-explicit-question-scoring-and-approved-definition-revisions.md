# Freeze explicit scoring rules with approved question definitions

Education fill-in questions use stable blank IDs and unique stem markers, with an explicit order, score, accepted texts and text comparison or manual rule. Repeated answers in different blanks remain separate. Subjective questions use stable manual scoring points and a reference answer. Each blank or point belongs to one confirmed curriculum mapping; its positive two-decimal score contributes once to the question total and that mapping's allocation. Multiple-choice scoring is explicitly chosen by the teacher. Format and unit instructions describe the accepted form; they do not imply an implemented formula or expression equivalence algorithm.

The project-owned, versioned `QuestionScoringDefinition` value is bounded JSON on candidates, questions and frozen paper questions. Drafts may be incomplete, but confirmation, submission, approval and new paper use validate the complete type-specific definition. Rules participate in request fingerprints and content comparison. Editing rules invalidates curriculum confirmation and pending approval. Existing enterprise objective questions without these rules retain their recorded behavior; new rules and subjective questions cannot enter the legacy objective-only task publication path.

Course and new-rule review decisions require the concurrency stamp the teacher actually saw and a stable operation ID. The committed decision has an immutable receipt, independent of the question's later state. Repeating the same request recovers that receipt; reusing an operation for different input fails. A stale page cannot approve a revised and resubmitted question. Functional review permission and the existing owner/source boundary both remain required; this does not introduce school delegation or settle PRD D05.

Approval stores an immutable `QuestionDefinitionRevision` in the same relational transaction, containing the full approved content, answers, analysis, scoring rules, curriculum allocations and actual source evidence, with reviewer and UTC time. The question points to its latest approved definition. New paper construction checks that this definition matches the current approved question, then freezes its revision ID and rules alongside the content. Editing a source question never rewrites an already saved paper or definition revision.

Teacher management returns the complete frozen rules. Student projections expose only the blank ID, order and stem marker needed for controls, in addition to the existing safe question surface. They do not return allowed answers, reference answers, scoring points, curriculum mapping IDs or private provenance through the new nested fields. Real structured answers, picture attachments, manual grading, teaching assignment, final release and WeChat delivery remain FR14–FR33 work; an independently saved paper does not publish a teaching task.

This extends [ADR 0012](0012-confirmed-material-mappings-and-recoverable-curriculum-generation.md) without changing its current-use authorization or [ADR 0009](0009-version-bound-school-sharing-and-source-provenance.md) provenance rules. D04 still governs the confirmed subject samples, formula and language comparison policy and attachment limits. Automated or SQL checks do not replace real curriculum, logged-in UI, provider or multi-host acceptance.
