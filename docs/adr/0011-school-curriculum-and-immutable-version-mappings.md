# Keep school curriculum versions immutable and teacher choices explicit

The first education curriculum catalog belongs to a school within the existing tenant and references the existing tenant-scoped subject. School ownership keeps one school's editors from changing another school's curriculum; it does not create a global publishing platform or grant access to personal knowledge. Maintenance requires the curriculum permission and an effective manager membership, while teachers read their school's published metadata and choose only for their own effective teaching assignments.

A textbook's publication edition and its catalog revision are different facts. Published textbook metadata, chapters and confirmed chapter-to-knowledge-point mappings are immutable; changes create another catalog revision. A course knowledge point has a stable school/subject identity and immutable named hierarchy revisions. Mappings reference a specific knowledge-point revision, not its current name. Teacher selections retain their assignment and exact textbook version; replacing a selection ends the old record instead of rewriting it. All references must agree on tenant, school, subject, grade and applicable teaching relationship.

Existing question knowledge-point strings and frozen examination snapshots remain unchanged. They are not automatically matched by name to this catalog. Later education generation and assessment must explicitly capture the selected textbook, chapters, stable point revisions and scoring mappings; grading history must read those snapshots rather than the current catalog. This catalog slice does not implement FR11's structured generation constraint or finish EDU-M2. Real textbooks and approved content are supplied under PRD D04; no sample publication content is invented by the product.
