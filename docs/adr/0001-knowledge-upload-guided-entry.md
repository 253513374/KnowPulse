# Separate guided knowledge upload from advanced knowledge management

KnowPulse will add `/knowledge/upload` as the primary guided entry for administrators who need to create one usable knowledge item from content, metadata, visibility, publishing, and indexing confirmation. The existing `/knowledge/items` page remains the advanced management surface for list maintenance, version lifecycle actions, attachments, feedback, and index troubleshooting, because forcing first-time upload and expert maintenance into one screen makes the knowledge-base workflow harder to understand.
