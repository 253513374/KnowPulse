# Stop subsequent student material reads after school-share withdrawal

Date: 2026-10-17. Status: accepted product decision. ADRs 0030 and 0031 implement the plan-activity and independent-assignment Web/service slices; ADR 0035 preserves the same boundary for notebook references. The 2026-10-18 progress entry records an authenticated HTTP/PostgreSQL independent-material withdrawal check; complete device/WeChat and concurrency acceptance remain separate. Resolves the student-material portion of PRD D03 and clarifies ADR 0009.

The user chose: “停止后续在线读取，保留历史答卷和已上交记录用于追溯”。Withdrawal therefore blocks every subsequent online body or attachment read that depends on the withdrawn school share, including a material version already assigned or previously opened by a student. An assignment or a prior delivery receipt is not a permanent grant to that source. Future material-read authorization must recheck the exact source/version and current effective share rather than rely only on a stored assignment snapshot or an old index.

Historical student answers, sealed exam submissions and explicit handed-in records remain for traceability under their own authorization; this does not reopen the source body or attachment. Existing frozen exam/result rules remain separate and are not silently changed into material-reading permissions. The decision does not recall previously seen/downloaded copies or settle other derivative-work, copyright, school-retention, leaving-school or account-recovery policies. Those remaining D03/D06 decisions retain their existing owners and gates.

Withdrawal does not invalidate an already authorized, published frozen assessment or rewrite its sealed answers and confirmed grades. Current authorized teachers continue grading, task-wide release and reasoned corrections under ADR 0017; old releases remain traceable. Student results still follow current identity and release gates and never grant source-material access. `EducationAssessmentSharedSourceRetentionTests.cs` exercises withdrawal both before grading and after release, including correction history and denied source reads; this extends the material-assignment withdrawal regressions without changing the decision.
