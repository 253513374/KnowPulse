# Keep curriculum evidence explicit across preparation, generation and saved papers

Education use resolves the teacher's effective assignment and exact textbook selection into an authorized snapshot of confirmed chapter-to-knowledge-point mappings. Names, free subject tags and model suggestions do not establish these relationships. Published catalog and point revisions retain the immutable semantics of ADR 0011. Reference reads remain untracked so looking up a catalog does not mutate its audit fields or concurrency stamp.

The material owner explicitly confirms an exact prepared version or indexed passage against a published mapping. Organizing personal material requires an effective teacher or manager school membership, but not an assignment. Whole-material confirmation is explicit. A specific passage mapping takes precedence within that textbook; ending it does not silently restore an older whole-material mapping. A school share freezes its confirmed binding IDs alongside the exact version and uses from ADR 0009, including ended passage binding IDs as evidence that blocks whole-material fallback. Ended bindings never authorize a hit. First sharing after an end and sharing again after withdrawal retain this exclusion; the 3,000-binding limit counts these IDs and fails closed. Later private organization does not change the shared curriculum, and new private passage bindings do not narrow an existing share's frozen scope. Ending a binding already present in that share still blocks its subsequent use.

Curriculum retrieval intersects these confirmed relationships with usable indexed versions and current source authorization before vector search, using existing chunk IDs without a second index or provider type in contracts. Every hit is checked again against relational facts. Subsequent adoption, review and paper construction retain the actual binding, mapping, version, chunk and share references. Removing editable display citations cannot remove this provenance. New use occurs in the same relational transaction as its saved fact: the existing school and account concurrency barriers protect assignment, selection and account changes, and source barriers interlock with material, binding and share withdrawal. Read-only history does not take these write barriers.

Personal preparations have immutable revisions of goals, curriculum, authorized material and question or paper references, and private reflection. Copying or newly using history requires current scope and source confirmation; history itself preserves its original evidence. Reflection is copied to a private material draft only by an explicit owner action. Student reports and private teacher observations are not automatically imported.

Generation creates a persisted pending task with immutable inputs and an idempotent operation ID. A host worker claims a run using its lease, heartbeat and concurrency token, reconstructs the active owner's permissions, and validates curriculum and source access before execution and again in the completion transaction. Failed-run retries and explicit new runs are distinct. New runs preserve edited and adopted candidates, and candidate edits use their content revision. Model-declared mappings and citation IDs remain visible for validation; unknown or unsupported IDs are not silently discarded into a valid result. A candidate retains the task's full curriculum snapshot while its proposed assessment subset remains separate.

Adoption produces a personal draft. The teacher separately confirms the actual assessment points, total score and allocation before review or new paper use; content or allocation changes invalidate that confirmation. Multi-point allocation does not duplicate the whole score. Monetary-style score precision follows the existing two-decimal scoring storage; normalized weights may retain more precision, but rounded allocated scores and their sum must agree with the confirmed total. Enterprise questions without explicit curriculum are not automatically reclassified.

Saving an education paper freezes the approved question content, curriculum, score allocation and original source evidence under an owner and recoverable save operation. It does not publish an ExamPlan or substitute organization assignment for education class assignment. Saved history can be recovered by its operation ID; a changed request cannot reuse that ID. Education task publication, student execution, grading evidence and the complete teaching loop remain the separate FR14–FR33 requirements in the PRD. Existing enterprise examination behavior keeps its recorded semantics.
