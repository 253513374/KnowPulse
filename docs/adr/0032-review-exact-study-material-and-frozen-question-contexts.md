# Review exact standalone study material and frozen-question contexts

Date: 2026-10-18. Status: accepted for the service and compiled Web slice. Extends ADRs 0019, 0020, 0022, 0024, 0030 and 0031. Authenticated browser, device and full application concurrency acceptance remain separate.

ADR 0031 deliberately treated relevant standalone material contact as Unverified and had no way to clear an exact context. This prevented silent independent-evidence claims, but also blocked legitimate new formal activities after ordinary study. Add a versioned review rather than treating every learning task as a hint or reusing an assessment/plan identifier.

## Authority and exact context

Four operations list candidate frozen questions, read one exact context, save a decision and recover the original operation. Each requires LearningManage, AssessmentManage and PersonalLibrary on its contract and explicitly in the service. Material-management permission alone cannot grant frozen answers. AnalysisManage and TeachingPlanView are not prerequisites. They share the existing study app service and current School, teacher and student account barriers.

The reviewer remains the study task's owner with current tenant, active account, recorded teaching and the fixed student's membership/enrollment authority. Candidate questions must belong to that teacher's personal saved paper, a published assessment revision assigned to the same student and the same term/class/teaching assignment, and overlap the study's confirmed positive knowledge-point scope. Listing uses a bounded 3,001-row read to enforce a 3,000-candidate limit. Read/recovery independently recheck the exact assignment; a list or known identifier is not authority. Another author's task is not opened by a graded-question link. Cross-author/exit-history policy remains governed by existing sharing and unresolved D06, not an implicit answer or private-task grant.

The student must actually have received the task body. Metadata lists, teacher progress and candidate discovery never write FirstProvidedAtUtc, start, completion or grade facts. A derived context key pairs the fixed study recipient with the exact immutable PaperQuestionId. It therefore fixes the delivered task publication and selected materials as well as the frozen stem, options, answer/analysis/scoring content and definition. A later task draft, replacement or another frozen copy of the same original question cannot replace that reviewed content. The context displays the original instructions, timing, expected workload and material references; it does not include later private drafts or the teacher's private handling notes.

## Decisions, recovery and materials

The existing three-valued use remains Unverified, Confirmed (answer/hint present) and ConfirmedNoHints. All writes require explicit comparison of the exact delivered version, selected attachments and frozen question, plus a private reason. Each append-only revision records predecessor, UTC time, expected stamp and exact command fingerprint. The parent advances atomically; stale writes and operation-ID reuse with changed content are rejected. A retry returns its original committed revision without replacing a newer decision. Old receipts still require current actor/tenant/assignment authority.

No new ConfirmedNoHints decision is allowed while an exact source or selected attachment authorization is unavailable. The normal material reader retains ADR 0030 checks; withdrawal does not grant a hidden copy. A reviewer may record Unverified or a known hint and recover a previous receipt after source withdrawal. Existing historical judgments are not erased when a material share is withdrawn. Review of an old task publication does not reopen that publication to student reads or new execution. The original first-provision time, rather than review time, remains the contact time.

The dedicated teacher page is available from student progress and the graded-question context list. It shows exact materials/frozen answers, current decision, private reason and read-only predecessors. It stores the original command in account/tenant/recipient-scoped sessionStorage before sending; receipt recovery/resend validates recipient, question, operation ID and command fingerprint. Abandonment does not erase a server commit. The temporary storage warning and navigation guards remain; this is not a confidentiality or device-erasure guarantee. Context/decision bodies and frozen answers are excluded from generic body auditing, and the service responses remain private/no-store. Students do not receive the decisions or private reasons through task/result DTOs.

## Evidence and formal start

A common internal, non-HTTP reader derives actual same-school/subject study contacts and their current paired decisions. It retains actual contacts after withdrawal/replacement/soft deletion and sends no material body or reason to analysis. Teacher source fingerprints include current decision IDs. Changed decisions invalidate captured source evidence; unrelated private reasons/IDs remain absent from the student's public version signals, following ADR 0022.

Standalone study contexts now carry an optional exact PaperQuestionId in EducationLearningQuestionExposure. Its null value is omitted from JSON, preserving legacy exposure bytes and fingerprints. Legacy assessment, feedback and plan exposures retain their original group-wide effect. Exact unknown study contacts apply only to the compared frozen question: a no-hints decision cannot clear a different frozen copy, nor can that copy's missing review incorrectly replace the reviewed decision. Confirmed answers/hints still apply to their known original/variant group. Source capture separately includes current confirmed study judgments for connected originals even when the judged frozen question is outside the selected assessment window; an exact no-hints judgment cannot erase that separate known hint. The analysis uses education-learning-evidence-v2-exact-context when scoped contacts exist; legacy inputs retain education-learning-evidence-v1 and their canonical property order.

Real formal-preview and student-start guards pass the exact frozen question IDs to novelty checking. ConfirmedNoHints clears only this material context, never a previous attempt, a real assessment review, another material context or known original relationships. Internal callers without frozen question IDs remain conservatively Unverified and cannot infer an exact decision. Exact context derivation replaces ADR 0031's original-only synthetic projection for new captures; old immutable source snapshots remain intact and no old decision is fabricated. Review creates no score, independent observation or mastery fact.

The combined teacher list sorts all context sources before paging and retains metadata-only access for contexts the teacher cannot review. Its merge reads only the necessary old-header range plus the bounded study contexts; it does not silently omit study contacts or change paging totals. StudyTask source value 4 is a projection label only; existing persisted assessment/feedback exposure constructors continue to produce only their original source kinds.

This slice reviews the exact task body/materials. Public change-notice texts and their separate actual-read provenance are not reclassified or cleared by this decision; broader notification-contact tracking remains separate work.

## Persistence and validation boundary

Migration 20261017170118_AddExactStudyMaterialHintReviews adds two tables without altering or backfilling old learning/grade facts. Restrict foreign keys pair fixed recipient/student/owner, frozen question/paper, current head and sequence, revision scope and predecessor; unique recipient/question and parent/sequence keys protect the version chain. The service/domain also enforce actual provision, published assignment, positive point overlap and teacher ownership. SQL constraints alone are not evidence of those semantic checks. Downgrade refuses any remaining review history rather than deleting it; compatibility/application rollback still needs an explicit review or restore.

SQLite integration and separate real PostgreSQL migration/constraint evidence do not prove full PostgreSQL application concurrency, Blob timing, logged-in browser behavior, WeChat or production capacity. Corrections, exception/resume policies, broader notifications, final teaching decisions and remaining PRD work stay open.
