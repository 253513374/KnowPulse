# Calculate explainable independent evidence and retain actual review exposure

Date: 2026-10-17. Status: accepted for the calculation and exposure slices; report integration remains required work.

FR29–FR33 require a proportion of valid attributed scores, not a probability of ability. `EducationLearningAnalysis.Calculate` handles one student, teaching assignment, subject, textbook version and UTC half-open window. It validates confirmed whole-question marks and conserving mapping scores before aggregating by exact knowledge-point revision. Multiple mappings to a point still count as one question. It does not merge different knowledge-point revisions or use activity completion, comments or an AI-generated percentage as evidence.

Evidence qualification is separate from grading. Unverified records do not become independent just because the score is confirmed. An independent qualification must refer to a real qualification revision and explicitly confirm original relationships, new requirements and no hints/answers. Practice and invalidation remain distinguishable from a genuine zero score. Subject rules carry a specific revision, window, minimum independent questions, assessment coverage, maximum evidence age and target. Without a configured rule, valid base ratios remain insufficient evidence; without valid evidence the ratio is null. Classification compares unrounded scores against the target.

Known original-question relationships form a deterministic connected group, including transitive and cyclic links. Different definition revisions of one original question already share that group. The first recorded encounter with a group is derived from complete minimal attempt history, including ungraded and abandoned attempts and history before the selected window. Subsequent copies or repeats cannot silently become new independent evidence. This history contains question identifiers and timing, not other students' answers. The application must validate the source scope; a derived grouping does not grant access to private question content.

Recorded answer/hint exposure at or before a submission excludes the group from independent evidence. Review after a submission does not invalidate the earlier measurement. `EducationQuestionExposure` retains a body-free, tenant/student-bound reference to the exact task recipient, paper question, original definition and official release. A feedback source also records its exact public feedback revision. Stable IDs and separate review/feedback unique indexes preserve first delivery times; corrected releases add facts without replacing earlier exposure.

A linked feedback delivery does not by itself prove every linked question's answer was present in the free text. Its source remains distinct: the calculator treats it as an unverified context, not a confirmed hint or practice. Establishing that it contained no hints requires a real review-revision reference; a qualification checkbox cannot silently erase that uncertainty. The entity-to-calculation value conversion preserves this distinction. Missing ledger records, especially before deployment, are not proof of no exposure; unverified evidence remains unverified until the required teacher workflow is completed.

The existing student result use case owns a relational unit of work. Only full review acquires the shared School/student-account barrier and rereads current authorization and release before constructing and recording the answer-bearing response. Hidden and score-only reads do not create exposure. Student feedback records all questions in its explicitly linked assessments only after the current version and all review gates are checked. Mere cards/notices do not create exposure. An exposure write failure prevents returning the answer-bearing response; the same transaction covers feedback's first-open fact. This records server delivery, not eye tracking, understanding or learning activity completion.

The concrete recorder is internal and explicitly excluded from conventional HTTP endpoints. It grants no reading permission. Its caller already verifies the current student/enrollment and holds the School/account barrier, while the recorder rechecks actor, tenant, exact current release and source associations. Foreign keys pair recipient, published task, release, paper question, original definition and feedback version; deletes restrict. The exposure table has no answer, feedback text, prompt or private material body.

The calculator returns a calculation version, rule revision, source fingerprint, window, exact fractions, missing-evidence reasons and per-grade inclusion/exclusion decisions. Identical replayed facts are deduplicated, conflicting duplicates or simultaneous old/new grade versions are rejected, and current corrections replace rather than accumulate. This pure result is not yet a persisted teacher report or student growth projection.

Derived numeric fingerprints normalize equivalent decimal scales using invariant, exact decimal formatting; `10`, `10.0` and `10.00` are the same score. This does not round marks, alter the raw submission or change grade revisions. Values that require more than two score decimals still fail evidence validation, including very small values formatted in exponent notation.

Qualification, original-relation and hint-context workflows are now integrated under [ADR 0020](0020-versioned-teacher-evidence-and-context-qualification.md). Remaining work includes source capture from authorized current grade versions, versioned subject-rule management, report jobs/retry/atomic snapshot switching, teacher class/individual reports, student score-open filtering, invalidation/recalculation and the personalized-plan/new-question reassessment loop. Domain tests, exposure persistence or teacher qualifications alone do not establish FR30/FR31, calibrated school thresholds, real WeChat clients, production concurrency or a complete teaching cycle.
